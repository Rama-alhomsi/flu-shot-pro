# Raw Data Dictionary (NHFS 2009)

## Targets
* `h1n1_vaccine`: 0 = No, 1 = Yes
* `seasonal_vaccine`: 0 = No, 1 = Yes

## Key Features
* `h1n1_concern`: Level of concern about H1N1 flu (0–3)
* `h1n1_knowledge`: Level of knowledge about H1N1 flu (0–2)
* `behavioral_*`: Preventive measures taken (0 or 1)
* `opinion_*`: Perceptions of vaccine effectiveness and risks (1–5)
* `age_group`, `education`, `income_poverty`: Demographic categorical indicators
